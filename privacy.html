Privacy Policy

Fulcrum Finance · a product of Fulcrum Foundry LLC
Effective date: June 11, 2026  |  Version 1.0

1.Introduction

This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) explains how Fulcrum Foundry LLC (“Fulcrum,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects information in connection with the Fulcrum Finance application and related services (the “Service”). Fulcrum Finance enables authorized users within an organization to connect that organization’s bank and credit accounts and to view read-only financial information through dashboards. By accessing or using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy.

2.Scope

This Policy applies to information processed through the Service. Fulcrum Finance is intended for business and organizational users who are authorized to access the connected financial accounts. It is not directed to the general public or to children. If you do not agree with this Policy, do not use the Service.

3.Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account and identity information: your name, email address, organization, and authentication identifiers used to access the Service.
  • Financial information (via Plaid): with your authorization, we access read-only financial data from your financial institutions through Plaid, which may include account balances, transactions, account and routing identifiers, account names and types, and account-owner information as provided by the institution.
  • Usage and technical information: log data, device and browser information, IP address, timestamps, and records of your interactions with the Service.

We do not collect or store your online banking username or password. Those credentials are entered directly into Plaid and are never visible to or stored by Fulcrum.

4.How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Service and its financial dashboards.
  • To authenticate users and secure the Service.
  • To communicate with you about the Service, including support and administrative messages.
  • To monitor, troubleshoot, analyze, improve, and protect the Service.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

5.Use of Plaid

We use Plaid Inc. (“Plaid”) to connect to your financial institutions and to retrieve financial data on your behalf. By using the Service, you grant Fulcrum Foundry LLC and Plaid the right, power, and authority to act on your behalf to access and transmit your personal and financial information from the relevant financial institution(s), in accordance with this Policy. You agree that your personal and financial information may be transferred, stored, and processed by Plaid in accordance with the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.

6.How We Share Information

We share information only as described below:

  • Service providers and sub-processors: cloud hosting and infrastructure providers (including Google Cloud Platform) that process data on our behalf under appropriate confidentiality and security obligations.
  • Plaid: as described in Section 5.
  • Within your organization: other authorized users of your organization’s account may access the connected financial data.
  • Legal and safety: to comply with applicable law, legal process, or enforceable governmental requests; to enforce our terms; or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Fulcrum, our users, or others.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to the protections of this Policy.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral or targeted advertising.

7.Data Retention

We retain personal and financial information only for as long as necessary to provide the Service or as required to meet legal, audit, or security obligations. Access tokens and connected-account data are deleted or invalidated when you disconnect a financial institution or close your account. We may retain limited records where required by law or for legitimate business purposes.

8.Data Security

We protect information using administrative, technical, and physical safeguards consistent with our Information Security Policy, including encryption of data in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest, encrypted storage of access tokens, least-privilege access controls, multi-factor authentication for administrative access, deny-by-default network controls, and continuous monitoring. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

9.Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. You may disconnect a financial institution at any time, which revokes our ongoing access to that institution’s data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at ryan@fulcrumfoundry.com. We will respond consistent with applicable law and will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

U.S. state privacy rights. Residents of U.S. states with applicable comprehensive privacy laws — including the Tennessee Information Protection Act — may have rights to confirm whether we process their personal information; to access, correct, or delete it; to obtain a portable copy; and to opt out of the sale of personal information or targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information or conduct targeted advertising. To exercise these rights, or to appeal a decision regarding a request, contact us at ryan@fulcrumfoundry.com.

10.Children’s Privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under 18 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it.

11.Third-Party Services

The Service relies on and may reference third-party services, including Plaid and your financial institutions. Their collection and use of information are governed by their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

12.International Users

The Service is operated in the United States. If you access the Service from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be processed and stored in the United States, where data-protection laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

13.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted with a revised effective date, and, where required by law, we will provide additional notice.

14.Governing Law

This Policy is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Tennessee, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

15.Contact Us
